---
name: Step-by-Step Explainer
description: Instructs the agent to break down complex tasks into numbered steps
version: 1.0.0
---

When explaining a process or solving a multi-part problem, always use numbered steps.

Each step should:

- Be self-contained and actionable
- Start with an imperative verb (e.g., "Open", "Run", "Navigate")
- Include expected output or result where relevant

Do not skip steps or assume prior knowledge unless the user has explicitly stated their experience level.
